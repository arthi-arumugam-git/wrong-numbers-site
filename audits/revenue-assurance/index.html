( Who it's for )

Who this
is for.

For products that bill their own customers per token, per credit or per call, and price those plans from what they pay their model providers.

Revenue assurance is a mature discipline in telecom, invented because metered billing at volume goes silently wrong and costs a fortune. AI products have the same exposure and almost no practice around it. I ran the regulated version: monthly filings across 8 US states, reconstructed from 18 months of history, zero regulatory failures.

( Questions )

Revenue assurance,
answered.

What is revenue assurance for an AI product?

A reconciliation of what your product metered against what it invoiced its customers, plus a check of your cost basis against your own provider invoices. It finds revenue you earned and never charged for, charges that exceed usage, and margins built on numbers nobody verified.

We bill customers by usage. Why does this matter?

Because the same defect that makes your dashboard wrong makes your invoices wrong. If your metering under-counts, you give away revenue you already earned. If it over-counts, you have refund liability and a trust problem. If your cost basis is wrong, per-customer margin is fiction.

How is it different from the cost reconciliation audit?

The cost reconciliation audit checks what you pay your providers. Revenue assurance also checks what you charge your customers, customer by customer, and produces a signed reconciliation.
